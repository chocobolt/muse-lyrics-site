Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Information processed

Muse Lyrics may process limited Discord information necessary to operate its commands, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message interaction data, and command usage data.

2. Music requests

When you use music commands, the bot may process the song title, URL, playlist identifier, or search query you provide in order to find and play music.

3. Third-party services

Muse Lyrics may interact with third-party services such as YouTube, Spotify, or other music providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process data according to their own terms.

4. Data storage

The bot may store limited technical data needed to operate, such as queue state, playback settings, cached track information, and error logs. The bot does not sell personal information.

5. Data retention

Technical and operational data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service or diagnose issues.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the bot, remove it from a server, or ask the server administrator to remove it. For data-related questions, contact the bot owner through the Discord server where Muse Lyrics is used.
